Numerical output match

Tier 3 · Reproducible - Gold core endorsement

Re-running the code on the deposited data reproduces the numerical results reported in the paper and appendix: estimates, standard errors, test statistics, sample sizes and numbers in the text. Sources differ on the tolerance, from exact agreement to small stated deviations.

Rating

TipMet

Re-running the code on the deposited data reproduces all numerical results in the paper and appendix (estimates, standard errors, test statistics, p-values, sample sizes, numbers in the text) exactly, or with differences only due to rounding or display format.

Based on #99/#103/#109, <1e-10 #20, within 1% #128, <=10% #95, +/-15% & p<=0.05 #144, direction + significance #107/#128, rounding/formatting = minor #134. CRA vs. CRR split: #138.

WarningPartially met

All results are obtained, but some differ beyond rounding while the conclusions are unchanged: same direction and statistical significance, and deviations within the tolerance (e.g. <=10% of the original value, or <=15% and p-values differing by no more than 0.05); or only part of the results can be reproduced (e.g. main tables but not the appendix).

ImportantNot met

The numerical results cannot be reproduced, or they deviate beyond the tolerance, or the direction or statistical significance of a focal result changes.
