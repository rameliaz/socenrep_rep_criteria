::: {.callout-note}
## Packages containing datasets that cannot be shared

This criterion is recommended in general. For packages that do not contain all datasets needed because some cannot be shared, it becomes a core criterion: without a third-party certification, only the Tier 1 criteria are assessed, so the package can get at most Bronze. With a certification, the core criteria that need the data are rated from the certificate, so Silver and Gold become possible. See rules 3 and 4 in [Rules of the badge system](../../pipeline.qmd#rules-of-the-badge-system).
:::

::: {.callout-note}
## Who counts as independent

An independent party is someone who is not an author of the paper and had no part in its data collection or analysis. The certificate or signed letter must state the reproducer's name, affiliation and relationship to the authors, what was reproduced, and how.
:::
